miLuka Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

miLuka is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and how to remove it. If anything here is unclear, contact us at the address below.

Information you give us

  • Account identity — your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account identity and display name. Used to create your account and keep you signed in.
  • Financial records — the transactions, wallets, budgets, recurring items and categories you enter or confirm. Used only to show you your own finances and to the people you explicitly share a wallet with.
  • Support messages — if you use “Report a bug”, the description you type plus the app version, device model, OS version and interface language. Reports are delivered to the developer by email and stored server-side. Please do not include passwords or account numbers in a report.

Notification access (optional)

If you choose to turn on notification capture, miLuka reads the text of notifications posted by the apps you explicitly select, on your device, to prefill a pending transaction for you to review. Nothing is saved until you confirm it. Only the transactions you confirm are sent to your miLuka account; pending captures you never confirm stay on your device. This access is optional and you can turn it off at any time in system settings. miLuka never reads notifications from apps you have not selected.

Where information is stored

  • On your device: your settings (theme, language, monitored apps), a local cache of your data for offline use, and unconfirmed pending captures.
  • On our server (Supabase): your account identity and financial records, so your data is available across sessions and to the people you share wallets with. Transport is HTTPS; access is restricted to you (and your wallet co-members) by row-level security.

What is never collected

  • Your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or files.
  • Your bank credentials. miLuka only parses notification text you have approved.
  • Advertising identifiers. miLuka contains no advertising or third-party trackers.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account from the app (Settings → Delete account), your profile and everything private to you — transactions, budgets, recurring items and wallets you own — is deleted from the server. In shared wallets, your contributions stay for the other members and ownership of the wallet passes to the longest-standing remaining member. Unconfirmed pending captures are removed when you discard them or uninstall the app.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. The only case where others see your data is a wallet you explicitly share: members of that wallet see its transactions, budgets and recurring items. Bug reports go only to the developer.

Children

miLuka is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
